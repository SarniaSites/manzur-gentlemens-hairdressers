    Manzur, Agnese and the team

    Manzur Asghar runs the shop at No. 1 Commercial Arcade with Agnese and the team. Ring to book, then tell us how you'd like it when you sit down.

    Every September the shop puts out a Manzur Legends XI for the Smaler's Charity Match, played in memory of local footballer Dan Smale. The money goes to MUG, who support men in Guernsey affected by male cancers.
